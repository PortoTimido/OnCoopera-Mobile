## Implementation Notes

- Mobile frames use a 390 px wide artboard baseline.
- Backoffice frames use a 1280 px desktop baseline, with some auth states wider (`1304`/`1383` px).
- The design includes both patient-facing mobile app flows and administrator-facing backoffice flows in the same Figma file.
- Duplicate frames with IDs under `308:*` appear to be copied/revised versions of earlier flows and should be checked visually before choosing them as source of truth.
- The major mobile navigation domains are: onboarding/auth, inicio, medicamentos, diario de sintomas, radar de apoio, artigos, relatorio medico, and configuracoes.
- The major admin domains are: dashboard, artigos, radar de apoio, interacoes medicamentosas, usuarios, configuracoes, and admin authentication.
